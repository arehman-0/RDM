IDM Replica extension test page

Served by node test/serve.mjs. Each section says what the extension should do.

1. Plain MP4 <video>

Popup lists sample.mp4 (VIDEO, ~2 MB). Hover the playing video → "⬇ Download" at its top-right.

2. blob: video (like MSE players)

The element's src is blob:. Popup lists the fetched blob-source.mp4 (sniffed from the network) and a "Page stream" entry; sending the page stream shows "This page streams its video through the player (blob:)…".

3. Dynamically inserted video (after 3 s)

After ~3 s the popup also lists dynamic.mp4.

(waiting…)

4. Video inside an iframe

Popup lists iframe.mp4; the floating button also works inside the frame.

5. Tiny video (filtered)

tiny.mp4 (< 100 KB, 1 s) must NOT appear.

6. File link (context menu / interception)

Right-click → "Download with IDM Replica". With interception ON (Options, min 1 MB), a normal click goes to the app instead of Chrome; with the app closed, Chrome downloads it normally.

Download test-2mb.bin (2 MB)

7. HLS stream (.m3u8)

The popup lists index.m3u8 (HLS) once — not its .ts segments. Hover the video → ⬇ Download → pick index.m3u8 (or "Download page video (best)"): the app downloads it with yt-dlp as one job and the card ends as an .mp4 of about 12 s. (If Chrome can't play HLS here, the video stays black; the playlist is still requested and listed.)
