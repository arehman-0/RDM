**Name:** IDM Replica — Download Helper
> Trademark note: "IDM" is the well-known abbreviation of Internet Download Manager.
> A reviewer may treat the name as confusing; consider a distinct product name before
> the first upload (it can't be changed freely afterwards without a new review).

**Category:** Productivity (Chrome) / Productivity (Edge)

**Language:** English

**Short description (≤132 chars):**
Send downloads and videos from any page to the IDM Replica download manager on your PC. Local only, no tracking.

**Detailed description:**

IDM Replica Download Helper connects your browser to the free IDM Replica download
manager for Windows (it must be installed; get it from the project's releases page).

- A Download button on the videos you play, including YouTube and many other sites.
- The toolbar popup lists the files and video streams the current page loaded.
- Right-click any link, image, audio or video → "Download with IDM Replica".
- Optional: take over Chrome's own downloads and get faster, resumable, multi-connection
  downloads in the app.

Privacy: the extension talks only to the app on your own computer (127.0.0.1). It has no
analytics or tracking, sends nothing to any other server and loads no remote code.

First use: click the toolbar icon → Connect to IDM Replica → click Allow in the app.

**Single purpose (Chrome form):** send the downloads and videos the user chooses to the
IDM Replica download manager on the same computer.

**Permission justifications:** see `permissions.md`.
**Privacy policy:** `privacy-policy.md` — must be hosted at a public URL (e.g. a page in
the repository or GitHub Pages) and that URL entered in both dashboards.
**Data-use disclosures (Chrome):** collects "Web history"? No. "Authentication info"? The
extension reads cookies for the download URL and passes them to the local app only; per
Chrome's wording this is handled, not collected — declare it under "Authentication
information" with "not sold, not used for unrelated purposes, not transferred off the
device". Certify the three Limited Use statements.
